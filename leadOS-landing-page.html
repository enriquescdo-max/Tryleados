Straight answers
The questions every agent asks first.
Is this TCPA compliant?
Yes — this is the core of the product. LeadOS enforces one-to-one prior express written consent before any AI voice call is placed. Every call has a timestamped consent record you can export. No consent on file = no call, period.
Will it sound like a robot?
Prospects are told they're speaking with an AI assistant from your agency. The voice is natural, and anyone who asks for a human gets flagged to you immediately.
What if the AI makes a mistake?
You review and approve the call scripts before launch, and every call is recorded and transcribed. You can pause the system with one click.
How long is setup?
One onboarding call. We connect your calendar and CRM, load your book, and verify consent status. Live within a week.
What does it cost?
Founding member rate: $500 setup + $497/month. First five agents only. Locks for life.
Does it replace my CRM?
No. LeadOS sits on top of EZLynx / HubSpot / InsuredMine and feeds them — it replaces the calls you're not making, not the tools you have.
